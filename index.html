Ecosystem Simulation

A solo Unreal Engine 5 project focused on creating a dynamic ecosystem simulation where predators, prey, and plants interact autonomously.

Unreal Engine 5 C++ / Blueprints Game AI

Description

Ecosystem Simulation is a solo research project developed entirely by me in Unreal Engine 5. The main goal of the project was to explore how biological ecosystem principles can be translated into interactive game systems and autonomous AI behaviors.

The simulation is based on interactions between three main elements of the ecosystem: plants, prey, and predators. Each unit operates independently and follows its own survival logic driven by hunger, movement, reproduction conditions, and environmental interactions. Prey search for plants while simultaneously avoiding predators, predators hunt prey to survive, and plants slowly spread across the environment over time.

One of the primary focuses of the project was creating a system where the world evolves dynamically without direct player control. Instead of scripted events, the ecosystem changes naturally depending on population growth, food availability, and chain reactions caused by the interaction between species. This allows every simulation to behave differently and creates unpredictable outcomes based on the initial conditions defined by the player.

The project also explores how ecosystem simulations can be used in game development to create more believable and immersive worlds. Dynamic ecosystems can improve replayability, support environmental storytelling, and introduce strategic gameplay elements by forcing players to adapt to constantly changing conditions.

During development, I designed and implemented the complete gameplay framework, AI behaviors, simulation logic, UI systems (setting starting condition of simulation). The project was created as both a technical experiment and a study of ecosystem-based systems in games.

Features

  • Autonomous prey and predator AI behaviors
  • Dynamic plant growth and spreading system
  • Reproduction and survival mechanics
  • Simulation speed control
  • Real-time ecosystem statistics visualization
  • Configurable starting conditions

Engine: Unreal Engine 5
Language: C++ / Blueprints

Flameborn – Gameplay Prototype

A fast-paced co-op prototype where four fire warriors defend a flame against waves of enemies, developed in a multidisciplinary team using Unreal Engine 5.

Unreal Engine 5 C++ / Blueprints Local Multiplayer

Description

Flameborn is a cooperative gameplay prototype where four fire warriors defend a central flame against waves of incoming enemies. The project focused on creating dynamic combat encounters and enemy AI behaviors that would constantly pressure players to balance defense, positioning, and teamwork.

I was primarily responsible for developing the entire enemy AI system using Unreal Engine Behavior Trees together with custom C++ systems and Blueprints integration. During development, I created three different enemy archetypes:

  • Ground Enemies – standard melee units focused on attacking players and overwhelming defenses.
  • Runner Enemies – fast units designed to avoid players and directly attack the central flame that explode in close contact.
  • Boss Enemy – a stronger enemy type with big attack range that increased pressure on the players.

One of the main technical challenges was designing AI decision-making systems that allowed enemies to dynamically choose between attacking players or targeting the central flame — the core objective players needed to protect. Enemies had to intelligently navigate around obstacles, react to changing situations, and continuously evaluate priorities during combat encounters.

Another significant challenge was implementing crowd control and movement management systems. Since large groups of enemies were active simultaneously, units frequently became stuck on one another during navigation. To solve this, I worked on crowd avoidance and movement optimization systems to improve enemy flow and maintain smooth large-scale combat behavior.

I was also responsible for creating the wave system that controlled enemy spawning, encounter pacing, and gameplay progression. Integrating the spawning systems, AI behaviors, combat logic, and gameplay balancing into the larger project architecture was one of the most complex parts of the development process.

Engine: Unreal Engine 5
Focus: Gameplay Programming, AI, Combat Systems
